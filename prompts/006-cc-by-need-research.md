# 006: Research which lukio subject most needs CC BY material, and what a full curriculum would take

## Expected answer, written before the work [M, the producer's reading given to the operator]

1. Need is greatest where the market fails: small student groups, costly adaptation, families with fewer resources.
   That is not the same as where most students are.
2. **Terveystieto** ranks high. TE1 is compulsory for all, content dates fast, students meet health
   misinformation, and public bodies (THL) publish free information that is not lesson-shaped.
3. **Psykologia** has less need: PS1 is compulsory and popular in the matriculation exam, but commercially well
   served, and an English CC BY base exists (OpenStax *Psychology 2e*), so it is the cheapest to build.
4. The direst need is in **S2** and the **small subjects** (minority religions, Sámi languages, sign language,
   Romani), where publishers have no market.
5. A full curriculum is about 20 subjects and a few hundred national modules, about 35 times the opinto-ohjaus work
   by machine. It needs a subject expert's review per subject, graded exercises with model answers, alignment with
   the digital matriculation exam, formulas, audio for languages, and media licensing.
6. Unknowns that could change the ranking: Opetushallitus may already fund material for the small subjects; since
   the 2021 extension of compulsory education, lukio materials are free to students, which moves the cost from
   families to the education providers; and past YTL exams may be reusable.

## The work

`scout.js` with six researchers: open materials per subject (aoe.fi and others); the commercial market per
subject; student numbers and exam participants per subject; Opetushallitus's own material support and the 2021
free-materials rule; YTL's past exams and the digital exam; international CC BY bases that could be adapted per
subject. Then the critic ranks the subjects by need with an explicit scoring, tests the expected answer, and proposes
a pilot.
