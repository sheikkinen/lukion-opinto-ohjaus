## Expected answers, written before the work [M]

1. **Curriculum.** LOPS 2019 (in force 1.8.2021) has two compulsory guidance modules worth 2 credits each: OP1,
   which covers starting upper secondary school, studying and learning, and wellbeing, and OP2, which covers further
   studies, working life and the future. The general aims include study skills, self-knowledge, career management
   skills, making a personal study plan, and choices for the matriculation examination and further studies. The
   law (Lukiolaki 714/2018) adds the student's right to personal and other guidance and to a personal
   study plan (henkilökohtainen opintosuunnitelma), and for some a further study plan (jatko-opintosuunnitelma). The
   transversal competences and the higher education studies (korkeakouluopinnot) in upper secondary school also
   touch guidance.
2. **Topics.** About 14–18 topics, split over OP1 and OP2, for example: starting upper secondary school and the
   school's practices; learning to learn and study skills; the personal study plan and choosing courses; wellbeing,
   time management and stress; self-knowledge and strengths; the matriculation examination and choosing its
   subjects; further study options (university, university of applied sciences, vocational, abroad); higher
   education admission: certificate-based selection and entrance exams; working life, skills and job seeking; career
   planning and career management skills; gap years, the army and civil service, and other routes; making the
   further study plan; money, housing and independent life after upper secondary school.
3. **Lesson form.** A 75-minute lesson is common, but schools vary (45, 60 or 75 minutes); a lesson plan in timed
   phases with options suits them all. Good guidance pedagogy is active and dialogic: reflection, pair and group
   work, tasks with real tools (Opintopolku, the certificate-selection points calculator, Työmarkkinatori,
   Ammattinetti), and the personal plan as a thread through all lessons.
4. **Self-study path.** Each topic needs a short text, an interactive task, a reflection prompt and a "next step",
   in a sensible order (OP1 in the first year; OP2 in the second and third). The self-study path cannot replace
   personal guidance; it says when to book a meeting with the counsellor.
5. **Sources.** ePerusteet (the curriculum text), Opetushallitus, Ylioppilastutkintolautakunta (YTL), Opintopolku
   and its admission rules, Vipunen statistics, Työmarkkinatori, Ammattinetti, Euroguidance, Ohjaamo, and the
   national guidance competence frameworks (e.g. the career management skills framework).
6. **Existing material.** Publishers sell guidance textbooks, and some schools and cities share open material.
   An open, curriculum-mapped backbone of full lessons seems rare, which is the gap this site fills.

## The work

Run `scout.js` with researchers on: the curriculum text (OP1, OP2, general aims, law); topics and their mapping;
guidance pedagogy and lesson design; self-study and digital guidance practice (privacy for minors); official sources
and facts that change yearly (admission, matriculation exam); existing open materials and accessibility. The critic
spot-checks, compares with the expected answers and proposes the topic list, the content contract and the site
structure.

## Limits

€0. Read-only on the web. No personal data. No text copied from textbooks or publishers.
