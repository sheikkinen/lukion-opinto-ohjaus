# Research report, brief 001, 2026-11-21

Seven agents ran: six researchers and one critic. The raw results, with URLs and grades, are in
`2026-11-21-scouting.json`. Grades: [A] read on the cited page and spot-checked by the critic where marked; [M] the
agents' or producer's reading.

## In plain words

- The curriculum has **two compulsory guidance modules, 2 credits each:** OP1 *Minä opiskelijana* and OP2
  *Jatko-opinnot, työelämä ja tulevaisuus*. They are marked passed or failed (S/H), not graded, and assessment rests on
  the student's self-assessment and the counsellor's feedback [A, spot-checked in the ePerusteet API].
- OP1 has 9 objectives and 8 content areas; OP2 has 10 and 8; the subject has 11 general aims [A]. The site will
  have **18 topics, 9 per module.** Each topic is one lesson and one self-study step, and every objective and content
  area is served by at least one topic [M, the critic's mapping].
- The law: the right to guidance is in Lukiolaki 714/2018 § 25 (with guidance in the year after graduation, § 25
  subsection 4) and the personal study plan (HOPS, three parts) in § 26 [A]. The brief's § 24 was wrong. One OPH page
  cites § 11; the site cites § 25 and § 26, as the curriculum does.
- A lesson plan is written for **75 minutes, with 60-, 45- and optional 90-minute variants.** The curriculum's own
  examples are 45 and 75 minutes (1 credit = 19 × 45 or 11.4 × 75 minutes) [A]. The four lessons a week are not set
  nationally, and neither are years and terms; the site shows timing as an example the school decides [A].
- **Facts that change yearly are linked, never copied:** exam dates, application windows, quotas, scoring tables,
  student aid amounts, call-ups [A for 2026 values; M for the rule]. YTL's enrolment deadlines (1.12 and 5.6) are
  stable but are linked too.
- **Privacy:** a static site with no accounts, analytics, forms, embeds or third-party fonts. Progress is stored in
  localStorage only when the student clicks, with a visible clear button. A short privacy notice mentions that GitHub
  Pages logs IP addresses [A for the scope of the digital services act; M for the localStorage reading, low risk].
- **Existing material:** commercial OP1 and OP2 books exist (Otava Etevä, verified; Studeo, likely). No openly
  licensed, curriculum-mapped set of full lessons was found, but aoe.fi could not be searched, so the gap is likely,
  not proven [A/M].

## Open, not verified

- The amendment attached to the curriculum record on 2026-05-22. The OP text in today's JSON matches the
  researchers' lists word for word.
- The career management skills frameworks (ELGPN, the Finnish 2023 proposal) and solution-focused guidance in Finnish
  teacher education. They may appear only as optional background, never in curriculum fields.
- Guidance for Finnish as a second language students. One fact is solid: from 2026, S2/R2 is scored like äidinkieli
  in certificate-based selection.
- URLs that could not be opened (Vipunen, Ammattinetti, Euroguidance, Studyinfo, siviilipalvelus.fi, aoe.fi): the
  build's link check must open them in a browser before they are used.
- The length of women's voluntary service and of civil service: link only, state no durations.
