# Which lukio subject most needs CC BY material: research report, brief 006

2026-11-21. Six researchers and one critic. The raw results, with sources, are in
`2026-11-21-cc-by-need-scouting.json`. Grades: [A] read on the cited page (the critic re-checked the key figures);
[M] estimate or reading.

## Answer in plain words

1. **S2 (suomi toisena kielenä ja kirjallisuus) is the greatest need this project can serve.**
   - 3,556 matriculation exam registrations in 2026, up from 2,367 in 2023 [A].
   - 11 modules, the same size as äidinkieli [A].
   - No commercial lukio series was found, and only two open module packages from 2020 exist (S21, S22) [A].
   - Opetushallitus's own small-group material names S2, but focuses on basic education and is priced, not open [A].
2. **Terveystieto or psykologia: terveystieto, narrowly.**
   - They tie on the scoring (10 points each).
   - Terveystieto's content dates fastest, and students meet health misinformation daily.
   - It is the larger exam: 14,567 registrations in 2026 against psykologia's 12,732 [A]. That number is a recovery
     to its 2017 level (13,514), not new growth [A].
   - It can be built from free public Finnish sources (THL, Ruokavirasto, UKK, Mielenterveystalo), restated in
     original words.
   - **Psykologia's shortcut is gone:** OpenStax *Psychology 2e* is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, not CC BY [A, spot-checked]. A
     translation could not be released as CC BY, so psykologia needs fresh writing too. It is the natural second
     subject; TE1's study wellbeing and PS4 *Tunteet ja mielenterveys* overlap.
3. **The smallest groups are the greatest need per student, but are only partly within reach.**
   - Catholic, Jewish, Buddhist, Bahá'í and Krishna religion: nothing was found from OPH, publishers or AOE.
   - The Sámi, Romani and sign-language mother-tongue syllabi have 11 modules each and almost nothing open. They need
     native-speaker authors and their communities, not machine-written material.
   - Orthodox and Islamic religion drop to mid-table: OPH now makes lukio material for both (priced, not open) [A].
4. **Open material is scattered everywhere.**
   - aoe.fi holds 5,462 materials [A]. 514 are CC BY or CC BY-SA and tagged lukio [A, researcher's count].
   - No complete open textbook series exists for any lukio subject. The nearest is the eight-part Russian *Aurora*.
   - The thinnest subjects: psykologia (0 psychology-only items), filosofia (0 open specific items), the religions
     (0), S2 (2), terveystieto (3, one TE1 course shell under CC BY-SA).
5. **Past matriculation exam tasks cannot be copied into CC BY material.**
   - YTL treats its questions as official statements without copyright, but third-party material in them must be
     cleared, candidate answers are confidential, and its pages say "Kaikki oikeudet pidätetään" [A].
   - The safe route is to link the tasks and write original tasks in the same style.
6. **Cost moved to the schools in 2021.** Lukio materials are free to students under the extended compulsory
   education. The providers pay, about €60 million a year for lukio by a 2020 planning figure [A for the figure; the
   statute text itself was not readable]. That makes good CC BY material a direct saving for municipalities and
   joint authorities.

## What a full lukio curriculum would take

- **Size:** ePerusteet counts 362 modules and 690 credits across all syllabi [A, read from the API]. A realistic
  majority set is about 22 syllabi, 130 modules and 246 credits.
- **Machine work, by analogy with this site** (4 credits took about 78 agents and 10 million tokens), times 1.5–2 for
  denser subjects with graded exercises, formulas and figures [M]:
  - the majority set: about 60 times this site, roughly 0.9–1.2 billion tokens;
  - every syllabus: about 170 times.
- **Human work, which machines cannot replace:** a subject-expert review per syllabus (about 22, or 40 for all), a
  medical or factual check for terveystieto, psykologia and biologia, and native speakers for languages and the
  minority syllabi.
- **Technical needs:** maths markup, audio for languages, media licensing per image, and exam-style practice tasks
  (optionally in the open MEX format, without the Abitti brand).
- **The order:** subject by subject, over many briefs, not one build. My earlier estimate of 35 times was too low.

## Proposed pilot: terveystieto TE1 *Terveys voimavarana* (2 credits, compulsory)

- **Scope:** about 8–9 topics on TE1's content (wholeness of health; nutrition, exercise, sleep, sexuality and mental
  health as resources; study wellbeing; stress). Each works as a lesson and a self-study step, with original
  exam-style tasks and model answers. Estimated 40 agents and about 5 million tokens.
- **Built on:** the ePerusteet TE1 text, THL, Ruokavirasto, UKK, Mielenterveystalo and Nyyti, restated in original
  words. The OPH support material and YTL exams are linked, not copied.
- **Reviewed by:** a terveystieto teacher (curriculum and exam tasks), a health professional (facts), and an
  inclusivity check of the sexuality and mental-health topics.
- **Proof it is useful:**
  - every TE1 objective covered;
  - no factual errors left after the review;
  - two or three outside teachers reuse a lesson;
  - a small student trial finishes the path;
  - uploaded to aoe.fi as CC BY 4.0;
  - actual cost measured against the estimate.
- **Fairness track, optional:** one S2 module from S23 onward, but only once an S2 teacher has agreed to review it.
