# Style guide: voice, terms and form

**Written for brief 002, 2026-11-21.** Every topic writer follows this guide and copies the form of the sample topic
`content/topics/07-ylioppilastutkinto.md`. The contract (`docs/content-contract.md`) says *what* sections exist; this
guide says *how* they are written. Where they disagree, the contract wins.

## 1. Voice

- **To the student (Opiskelijalle, Tehtävät, Pohdinta, Itseopiskelun askel, Milloin opinto-ohjaajalle): `sinä`.**
  Warm, calm, respectful, never chummy or preachy. Speak to a capable 16-year-old who is reading alone at home.
  - Yes: *Sinun ei tarvitse tietää vielä kaikkea. Riittää, että teet ensimmäisen version.*
  - No: *Nyt kannattaa todella skarpata!* / *Opiskelijan tulee huolehtia…* (no slang, no third person, no "tulee").
- **To the counsellor (Tuntisuunnitelma, Eriyttäminen): collegial, short, practical.** Imperative or passive, no
  lecturing about pedagogy. You are one colleague handing another a plan that works tomorrow.
  - Yes: *Näytä YTL:n sivu ja kysy: mikä tässä yllätti?* / *Jos aika loppuu, siirrä pohdinta kotitehtäväksi.*
  - No: *Opettajan on tärkeää muistaa, että dialogisuus on ohjauksen ydin.*
- **Encourage, don't promise.** Say what the student can do and where to check, never that something "will surely"
  work out. Uncertainty is normal: *Suunnitelma saa muuttua.*
- **No praise or filler** (guideline §4, norm ledger row 7): no *Hyvä kysymys*, *Kiitä rehellisyydestä*, *rohkeaa*,
  and no *Muista, että…* unless the reminder carries information. Plain, matter-of-fact Finnish.
- **The plan belongs to the student.** Offer options and questions, not verdicts. No "kannattaa ehdottomasti".

## 2. Plain Finnish (selkeä yleiskieli)

- One idea per sentence. Aim for sentences under 15 words; break anything over 25.
- Verbs, not nouns: *ilmoittaudut kokeeseen*, not *ilmoittautumisen suorittaminen*.
- Active voice to the student: *Rehtori lähettää hakemuksen*, not *Hakemus lähetetään*.
- Explain a term the first time it appears in a topic, in the same sentence or the next:
  *Tutkintokerta tarkoittaa yhtä koekautta, kevättä tai syksyä.*
- Paragraphs of 2–4 sentences. Lists for steps and rules; prose for reasons and encouragement.
- Numbers: write small stable counts as words (*viisi koetta, kolme tutkintokertaa*). Never type a changing figure
  (see §8).
- Headings below H2 are short nouns or questions: `### Mistä tutkinto koostuu?`
- No English loans when a Finnish word exists (*palaute*, not *feedback*; *tehtävä*, not *task*).
- Selkeä yleiskieli is not selkokieli. For selkokieli and S2 support, add a short glossary in Eriyttäminen (§7).

## 4. "Osaan" outcomes

- Section `## Opiskelijalle` opens with the line `**Tämän aiheen jälkeen**` and a list of 3–5 outcomes.
- Every outcome starts with **Osaan** (or *Tiedän* / *Ymmärrän* only when "osaan" is impossible; prefer *Osaan*).
- One outcome per cited curriculum code at least; each mirrors the *meaning* of its objective or content area in
  the student's own words. Never copy curriculum wording: the build prints the exact text in Lähteet.
- An outcome is something the student can check alone: *Osaan tarkistaa YTL:n työkalulla, täyttääkö koeyhdistelmä
  vaatimukset.* Not: *Osaan ylioppilastutkinnon.*
- The self-assessment in `## Pohdinta` repeats the same outcomes, word for word, on a four-step scale:
  *En vielä · Osittain · Melko hyvin · Hyvin*.

## 5. Tasks (`## Tehtävät`)

Each task is an H3 with a number and a verb-led title, then four fixed bold lines and numbered steps:

```
### Tehtävä 1: Kokeile koeyhdistelmää
**Aika:** 15 min
**Tarvitset:** puhelimen tai tietokoneen, muistiinpanot
**Ilman laitetta:** (one or two sentences: how to do the same on paper)
1. …  (at most 5 steps, each one action, imperative, sinä)
```

- At least one task per topic uses a real official tool or page by a stable link (not a yearly sub-page).
- Steps end in something the student *keeps* (a note, a draft, a question), never in something sent to the site.
- No model answers and no "oikea vastaus". OP is assessed S/H on self-assessment.
- Case tasks use clearly fictional first names, mixed and neutral (*Alex, Sanni, Niko, Ilona, Eemil, Mira*), marked
  *kuvitteellinen*. Cases never tie a field to a gender or background, and at least one case in the topic takes a
  route other than university.

## 6. Lesson tables (`## Tuntisuunnitelma (75 min)`)

- Columns, always in this order: `| Vaihe | Min | Toiminta | Ohjaajan rooli | Vaihtoehto | Jos aika loppuu |`.
- `Min` holds a bare integer. No total row: the build adds up the column and checks it against the heading.
- The 75-minute phases follow the contract template (5 / 10 / ≤10 / 25 / 10 / 10 / 5). Use the template names.
- `### 60 min`, `### 45 min` (and optional `### 90 min`) are full tables in the same columns, not prose.
- `Toiminta` says what students do; `Ohjaajan rooli` what you do; `Vaihtoehto` another way (paper, pair, quieter);
  `Jos aika loppuu` what to cut or move to homework. A dash `–` when nothing applies.
- After the tables, in this order: `### Valmistelu` (at most five lines), `### Avoimet kysymykset` (5–8 questions
  that cannot be answered yes or no), `### Pelisäännöt ja oletusten tarkistus`.

## 7. Inclusive, non-segregating guidance

- Every route is equal: yliopisto, AMK, ammatillinen koulutus, kansanopisto, välivuosi, asepalvelus tai
  siviilipalvelus, työ, opiskelu ulkomailla. Name several in the same breath; never rank them.
- No examples that link a field, subject or exam to gender, origin, family or disability (*no "pojat ja pitkä
  matikka"*). Gender-aware guidance means noticing and questioning such assumptions, not repeating them.
- Do not assume family resources, a home computer, Finnish as the first language, or two parents.
- Never require disclosure. Reflection is private: *Kirjoita omaan muistioosi*, never *kerro ryhmälle*. Group work uses
  fictional cases, not the students' own situations.
- Wellbeing, learning difficulties and S2: link the school's and the public services (oppimisen tuki, opiskeluhuolto,
  Ohjaamo) where the content needs them. No reassurance formulas (*kysyminen ei ole heikkoutta*), no help or crisis
  blocks, no 112 or emergency-number lines, no helplines as help pointers (guideline §4, norm ledger rows 3 and 7,
  D-015). Do not diagnose or label.
- Every lesson plan ends with a short `Oletusten tarkistus` the counsellor reads before teaching.

## 8. Facts that change: link them

- **Stable rules may be stated** (e.g. *vähintään viisi koetta; äidinkieli ja kirjallisuus on kaikille pakollinen*).
- **Changing values are never typed**: dates, deadlines, fees, euro amounts, percentages, quotas, points, call-up
  dates. Write the rule and link the value: *Tarkista [koepäivät YTL:n sivulta](https://…/koepaivat).*
- Even values that seldom change (e.g. YTL's enrolment deadlines) are linked, not typed.
- Link to a stable parent page, not a yearly sub-page (`/koepaivat`, not `/koepaivat/kevaan-2027-koepaivat`).
- Every body link also appears in `official_links` with `read: YYYY-MM-DD` from a page you opened. In
  `## Milloin opinto-ohjaajalle` write the read date inside the link text too: *[Etsi lähin Ohjaamo (luettu
  21.11.2026)](https://ohjaamot.fi/lahin-ohjaamo)*.
- List the facts in `yearly_facts` using keys from `data/yearly-facts.yaml`.
- School practice (own deadlines, Wilma forms, course trays, which year a plan is made) goes into `school_adapts`
  and is called an example in text: *Koulusi kertoo oman takarajansa (esimerkki: Wilma-lomake).*
- If a page cannot be opened after two tries, link a parent page you could open, or leave it out and report it.

## 10. Length and section habits

- Whole topic 2,000–3,500 words; `## Opiskelijalle` 300–600 words.
- `## Itseopiskelun askel` uses four bold labels in order: **Tavoite**, **Aikaa kuluu**, **Näin etenet** (a numbered
  list: lue → tee yksi tehtävä → pohdi → seuraava askel), **HOPSiin** (which part, what to write). Do not draw the
  "Merkitse tehdyksi" tick; the site does.
- `## Eriyttäminen` uses bold run-in labels: **Helpompi polku**, **Syvempi polku**, **S2 ja selkeä kieli** (with a
  mini glossary), **Lukeminen, keskittyminen ja jännitys**, **Ilman laitetta**, **Tuki**.
- `## Milloin opinto-ohjaajalle`: a list of concrete triggers in sinä form, then **Apua saat myös** with dated links
  to services that match the triggers (for example Ohjaamo, opiskeluhuolto). No 112 or *soita hätänumeroon* line, no
  crisis line, no "Jos olet heti vaarassa" block (D-015). No closer that praises the question (*se on hyvä kysymys
  seuraavaan ohjauskeskusteluun*).
- `## Lähteet` contains only one line: `Lähteet kootaan sivun tiedoista automaattisesti.` The build fills it.

## 11. Never

- Never type curriculum wording, or present school practice as a national requirement.
- Never type a yearly figure, or a date or euro amount near a yearly fact.
- Never ask for personal data, health information, grades or family details to be shared with the site or the group.
- Never stereotype, rank routes, or suggest a "right" choice.
- Never copy text from textbooks or other publishers. Quote official texts at most one short sentence, with source.
- Never use real private people's names, or names of real students or staff.
- Never embed images, scripts, fonts or iframes from other hosts; plain links only.
- Never promise an outcome (admission, a grade, a fixed deadline) the official source does not promise.
