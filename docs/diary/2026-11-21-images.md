# Brief 003: images and diagrams, accepted with minors

**Date:** 2026-11-21 **IDs:** brief 003, D-007, D-009

## What happened

The art director planned 7 diagrams and 21 illustrations while a tool builder wired game-engine's pimage modules
into `tools/images.py`. The 7 Finnish diagrams came out right on nano-banana. The illustrations failed on klein:
drive E: was full and ComfyUI could not write its job files. The judge rejected. The producer switched the
illustrations to p-image ($0.005 each, within "klein or pimage", D-009); the image checker failed 4 of 21, three
passed after a remake, one still shows six scenes for a four-scene prompt. Total spend $0.40 of €10.

## Traps

- **A shared machine's disk is part of the toolchain.** A full scratch drive stopped the free local model.
- **Montage prompts.** p-image turns "four scenes" into six; counts in prompts are not obeyed.

## Heuristic

Ask an image model for one scene, not a montage; put counts in a diagram, where the checker can read them.

**Seed:** Should the image tool check free disk space on the GPU host before it plans a klein run?
